Privacy Policy

Digital Khata
Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy explains what Digital Khata does with information when you use the Android app. Digital Khata is a personal notebook for income, expenses, savings, budgets, bills, and shopkeeper credit. It is not a bank and it is not formal accounting software.

The developer is inam Ullah. Contact: inamuu5434@gmail.com.

Account

You sign in with an email address and password before you can use the app. The account is created and checked through Google Firebase Authentication. We use the email address to open your khata, keep your records with that account, and let you sign in again on a phone.

Information you enter

The amounts, categories, notes, dates, accounts, savings goals, budgets, bill reminders, and shopkeeper credit that you type are stored in Google Cloud Firestore, in a private area for your user id. Other people cannot read those records. We use them only to show your khata, calculate totals, and make the PDF or Excel file you choose to export.

The app does not connect to your bank and does not ask for a bank password, card number, or national identity number.

Photos

If you attach a receipt photo, that photo stays on the phone. It is not uploaded with the cloud backup.

Microphone

The microphone is optional. If you use it, your phone’s speech service turns speech into text. Digital Khata does not send that text to a separate paid AI service, and it does not keep the recording. Nothing is saved until you review the text and tap Save.

Lock on this phone

A PIN or fingerprint lock, if you turn it on, stays on that phone. The PIN is stored as a salted hash, not as the original digits.

What leaves the phone

Your email and the records you save are sent to Firebase, which is operated by Google, so the app can sign you in and keep your khata. The connection uses HTTPS. Google processes that data as the service that hosts authentication and the database. We do not sell your information, and the app has no advertising.

Signing out removes the saved copy of your khata from that phone. The account copy remains in Firebase until you delete the account.

How long it is kept

Records stay in your account until you delete them or delete the account. To delete the account and the cloud copy, open More, then Backup and Restore, then Account, then “Delete account and cloud data”.

Children

Digital Khata is not made for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
