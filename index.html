Privacy Policy

Digital Khata
Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains what Digital Khata does with information when you use the Android app. Digital Khata is a personal notebook for income, expenses, savings, budgets, bills, and shopkeeper credit (udhaar). It is not a bank and it is not formal accounting software.

The developer is inam Ullah. Contact: uinam5434@gmail.com.

Account

You sign in before you can use the app. You can create an account in one of two ways:

  • Mobile number: your name, your country calling code and mobile number, and a PIN of 4 to 6 digits that you choose. No SMS code is sent, so we do not check that you own the number.
  • Email: your name (optional), your email address, and a password.

The account is created and checked through Google Firebase Authentication. Firebase gives your account a user id. We use your mobile number or email to sign you in, your user id to keep your records with your account, and your name to greet you in the app and to sign the khata statements you choose to send. Your PIN or password is checked by Firebase. We never see it and it is not stored in the app.

You stay signed in on your phone until you sign out.

Information you enter

The amounts, categories, notes, dates, accounts, savings goals, budgets, bill reminders, and shopkeeper credit that you type are saved on your phone and backed up to Google Cloud Firestore, in a private area for your user id. Other people cannot read those records. We use them only to show your khata, calculate totals and reports, remind you about bills, and make the PDF or Excel file you choose to export.

If you add a customer or supplier, the name, mobile number, and note you enter for them are saved in your khata in the same way. We use that number only to start a call or a WhatsApp message from your phone when you tap Call or WhatsApp. We never contact them ourselves.

The app does not connect to your bank and does not ask for a bank password, card number, or national identity number.

Sharing statements

When you tap Share or WhatsApp, the app prepares a text statement and opens WhatsApp or your phone's share menu. You decide whether to send it. What happens after that is covered by the policy of the app you send it with.

Photos

If you attach a receipt photo, that photo stays on the phone in the app's private storage. It is not uploaded with the cloud backup, and it is removed if you uninstall the app.

Microphone

The microphone is optional. If you use it, your phone's speech service turns speech into text. Digital Khata does not send that text to a separate AI service, and it does not keep the recording. Nothing is saved until you review the text and tap Save.

Kept only on this phone

  • Your chosen language, and the mobile number or email you last signed in with, so the sign-in screen can fill it in for you. Your PIN or password is not kept.
  • An app lock PIN or fingerprint lock, if you turn it on. The PIN is stored as a salted hash, not as the original digits.
  • Bill reminders are scheduled as notifications on the phone.

What leaves the phone

Your name, your mobile number or email, your user id, and the records you save are sent to Firebase, which is operated by Google, so the app can sign you in and keep your khata. The connection uses HTTPS. Google processes that data as the service that hosts authentication and the database. We do not sell your information, we do not share it with anyone else, and the app has no advertising and no analytics.

Before you sign out, the app saves your entries to your account and warns you if something could not be saved. Signing out then removes the copy of your khata from that phone. The account copy remains in Firebase until you delete it.

How long it is kept

Records stay in your account until you delete them or delete the account.

When you delete an entry in the app, it disappears from your khata and totals straight away. So that you can undo it, it stays in your account marked as deleted and can be restored from More, then Recently deleted, for 30 days. To have deleted entries or any other data removed completely while keeping your account, follow the steps on the data deletion page.

Deleting your account

To delete your account and all of its data, open the app, tap More, tap your name at the top, then tap "Delete my account and all data". This deletes the account and the cloud copy right away. If you cannot use the app, email uinam5434@gmail.com and we will delete it within 7 days. The steps are also on the account deletion page.

Children

Digital Khata is not made for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
